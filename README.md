# BusinessPilot — AI Business Assistant

A beginner-friendly, mobile-ready starter app for planning and managing a small business.

## Included in Phase 1

- Business dashboard
- Business idea starter
- Marketing post draft generator
- One-page business plan builder
- Income and expense tracker
- Customer reply draft helper
- Progressive Web App manifest and basic offline caching

## Run the app

The easiest free hosting option is GitHub Pages:

1. Open this repository on GitHub.
2. Select **Settings**.
3. Select **Pages** in the left menu.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Choose the `main` branch and `/(root)`, then save.
6. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish the site. The published address will appear on the Pages settings screen.

You can open the published site on an Android phone or computer. On Android, use the browser menu and choose **Add to Home screen** if available.

## Important first-version limits

- The writing helpers use simple built-in templates. They do not yet connect to a live AI model.
- Money entries are stored in the browser's local storage on the device being used. They are not synced or backed up.
- Do not enter sensitive financial or customer information.
- This starter app does not guarantee income. Validate demand before spending money.
